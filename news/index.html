    Tara Mishra publishes his first paper, in Physical Review Materials

    Feb. 5, 2021 by Piero

    On February 5th, the research work of Tara P. Mishra entitled Unlocking the Origin of Compositional Fluctuations in InGaN Light Emitting Diodes was published in Physical Review Materials. Tara developed a powerful methodology to detect the formation of In clusters in high Indium content InxGa1-xN LEDs quantum wells (QWs). His study showed a direct comparison between the computationally predicted and experimentally observed compositional fluctuations.

    Our review is on the cover of Nature Materials

    Nov. 22, 2019 by Piero

    Our review “Fundamentals of inorganic solid-state electrolytes for batteries”, published in Nature Materials in August, has been chosen for the cover of the journal’s December issue. The review, written with colleagues at the University of Bath and LRCS in Amiens, surveys how ions move through inorganic solid electrolytes, their electrochemical and mechanical properties, and the challenges that remain before solid-state batteries become practical.

    Our review of solid electrolytes for batteries appears in Nature Materials

    Aug. 19, 2019 by Piero

    Our review “Fundamentals of inorganic solid-state electrolytes for batteries” is out in Nature Materials. Written with Theodosios Famprikis, James Dawson, Saiful Islam and Christian Masquelier, of the University of Bath (United Kingdom) and LRCS in Amiens (France), it describes what is known about the inorganic solid electrolytes at the heart of solid-state batteries: how ions move through them across length scales, their electrochemical and mechanical properties, and how they are processed.
