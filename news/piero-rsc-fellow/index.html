    Piero Canepa elected a Fellow of the Royal Society of Chemistry

    May 31, 2021 by Piero · 1 min read

    Piero Canepa, Assistant Professor in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at the National University of Singapore, has been elected a Fellow of the Royal Society of Chemistry (FRSC).

    Fellowship is one of the most prestigious distinctions the Royal Society of Chemistry confers. It is open to the Society’s members and recognizes an outstanding contribution to the advancement of the chemical sciences, sustained through publications, discoveries and other achievements; candidates are proposed with references from existing Fellows. Fellows use the letters FRSC after their names. More about the Fellowship is on the Royal Society of Chemistry’s website.
