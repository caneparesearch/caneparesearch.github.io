Vishakha Kapoor

FYP Undergraduate Student

I am now a Research Scientist in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at the National University of Singapore.

I was a Year 4 undergraduate student in Materials Science and Engineering at the National University of Singapore. I was passionate about sustainability, and I was particularly interested in solar energy and battery materials. I was excited to learn about the application of computational techniques to the study and development of sustainable energy technologies. In my free time, I love to read, do crossword puzzles, explore new sudoku variants, and play survival and sandbox games.
