I received my Bachelor’s degree in Chemical and Biological Engineering & Physics from Seoul National University in August 2019, and my Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from the University of California, Berkeley, in August 2025. Under the guidance of Prof. Gerbrand Ceder, my doctoral research focused on the phase evolution of multivalent cathodes and the accelerated high-throughput screening of multivalent-ion solid-state conductors using machine learning interatomic potentials. My broader research interest lies in leveraging machine learning potentials to simulate larger and longer timescale systems that were previously inaccessible.

In my free time, I enjoy running.
